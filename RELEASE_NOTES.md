# Shrinkium 0.1.0

First full Release for Hytale 0.7.

- Crystal-powered Shrink Ray with 75%, 50% and 25% target sizes, aimed shots and SELF controls.
- One crystal per successful size change, paid original-size restoration, and free emergency self recovery.
- Cave-grown Shrinkium Crystal Ore, now with deposits on all six sides, and the standard Workbench ray recipe.
- Original ray/crystal models, painted textures, native controls and generated sound effects.

Compatibility: **>=0.7.4 <0.8.0**, compiled and native-tested with **Hytale 0.7.4**. CurseForge’s 0.7 tag does not certify every patch.

Stop normally, back up world/player data and place **Shrinkium-hytale0.7-0.1.0.jar** in Mods; enable it for the world and restart. The asset pack is included. Restore affected players/creatures before uninstalling.

Known limits: real second-client multiplayer and the complete ordinary survival acquisition route remain unverified. After emergency self restoration, reselect the ray if its held model disappears. Ore appears only in newly generated eligible caves; terrain generation may pause. No shrinking blocks or growth beyond original size.
