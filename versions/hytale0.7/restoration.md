# Leave room to grow

Return to open ground before restoring your original size. A low ceiling or nearby
solid blocks can prevent safe growth. The same clearance rules apply whether the
target is a player or an animal.

## Paid ray restoration

Open Secondary, choose **RESTORE ORIGINAL**, then use **SELF** or close the panel and
fire at the intended living target. A successful restoration costs one crystal.
Blocked growth refuses without charge or pauses until space becomes available.

## Free emergency self recovery

Use the native chat command:

    /shrinkium restore

It restores only yourself and works without a ray or crystals. When the current
space cannot hold your original size, the mod can use its checked saved pre-shrink
position in the same world, if that location is still loaded and safe. It does not
teleport through to an arbitrary world or guarantee recovery at an unsafe anchor.

Move clear when possible, keep the world backup and report a refused recovery with
the terrain conditions. Do not force growth by repeatedly firing into a blocked space.

## Saved state and the current visibility issue

Normal saving-world reload has retained the original baseline, selected size and
current miniature scale in native checks. Broader lifecycle and multiplayer checks
remain unfinished. Use a world that actually saves players.

After free restoration the held ray may disappear even though it remains in the
hotbar. Select another hotbar slot and then select the ray again. This recovers the
visible model in the observed case; the underlying camera/held-item issue is still
open. Avoid removing the mod while entities remain miniature.
