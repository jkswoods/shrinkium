# From cave crystal to ray

| Result | Route | Quantity or cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shrinkium Crystals | Mine Shrinkium Crystal Ore | 2 crystals per ore |
| Shrink Ray | Workbench → Tools | 6 Iron Bars  + 4 Copper Bars  + 2 crystals; 4 seconds |
| Successful size change | Aimed ray or SELF | 1 crystal |
| Paid original restoration | Select RESTORE ORIGINAL, then activate | 1 crystal |
| Emergency self restoration | /shrinkium restore | Free |

Crystal Ore decorates newly generated default-world **Rock Stone** cave faces. It does
not spawn in every cave, rewrite explored terrain or create a new dimension. The
ordinary acquisition/crafting loop still needs a complete native playtest.

The ingredients are ordinary stock Iron and Copper Bars plus the mod's crystals.
There is no separate machine, fuel refinery or Shrinkium workbench. No ore-smelting
step or rarer material gate is required by the recipe.

Keep one activation crystal beyond the two used in crafting. Misses and same-size
no-ops do not spend it. Crystals do not power an idle ray and are not consumed over
time while you remain small.

The item IDs are **SH_Ray**, **SH_Crystal** and **SH_Ore_Stone**. They can help identify
an asset when reporting a problem; the player-facing names are Shrink Ray, Shrinkium
Crystal and Shrinkium Crystal Ore.
